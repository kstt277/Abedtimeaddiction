03 / CONTEXT & METHODS

Read the patterns
in context.

The supplied dataset, The Bedtime Addiction: Sleep Debt & Screen Time, contains 8,500 simulated individual records and 18 columns, including an identifier. The original project identifies Kaggle as its source.

Charts use the supplied CSV. The model calculations reproduce the two formulas in the supplied R Markdown file, with explicit reference groups. Filters apply to descriptive charts. Boxes follow the conventional 1.5 IQR rule, with quartiles calculated by linear interpolation.

Observed differences may reflect the assumptions used to generate the data. Associations support exploration within this dataset, rather than causal conclusions about real people.
